About Me

From healthcare to code, driven by curiosity, built on discipline.

My Story

I spent years as a Doctor of Chiropractic — diagnosing problems, building trust with patients, and finding solutions under pressure. What I didn't expect was how naturally those same skills would translate to software engineering. The ability to break down a complex problem, trace it to its root cause, and communicate a clear solution is something both fields demand.

My transition into tech wasn't accidental. I was drawn to the idea that software lets you build things that scale — tools that can help thousands of people at once, not just one patient at a time. That realization pushed me to go all in, and I have not looked back since.

I have completed the MIT xPRO Professional Certificate in Software Engineering and continue building real full-stack applications, studying applied AI, and sharpening my skills every single day.

What Drives Me

Passion for Learning

I don't just learn what I need to get by — I go deep. Whether it's a new framework, a design pattern, or an entirely new language, I treat every gap in my knowledge as an opportunity, not a setback.

All-In Work Ethic

I don't do things halfway. From building my first Flask app to architecting a full MERN stack application, I put the same level of care and effort into every line of code. Good enough is never the goal.

People-First Mindset

Years in patient care taught me to listen first and solve second. I bring that same approach to software — building things that are intuitive, accessible, and genuinely useful to the people who use them.
